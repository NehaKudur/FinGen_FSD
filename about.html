About FinGen

FinGen is a retro-futuristic financial literacy platform that blends games and practical lessons. Our mission is to make money management fun, accessible, and engaging for everyone.

Vision

For every user to walk away with stronger financial habits through interactive gameplay and applicable knowledge.

How It Works

  • Play games to learn savings, budgeting, debt, investment, and risk management.
  • Track progress through levels, score, and dynamic challenges.
  • Use the quiz to confirm your knowledge and unlock advanced content.
